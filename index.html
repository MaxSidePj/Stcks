How suggestions work

Choose your weekly run count in Settings. This week starts from your usual distance and longest recent run, then adjusts remaining sessions after each run. Missed kilometres are never added to later sessions.

Later weeks are an indication of the road to your race: the long run grows by at most 1 km a week, total volume by at most 8%, every fourth week is lighter, and the final two weeks taper. One run a week adds strides, intervals or a tempo section once your long run allows it. Dates are suggestions; run on any available day. The whole plan is recalculated from what you actually log.

A small increase is considered only after two complete logged weeks with manageable recorded effort and no reported pain. Check-ins, lighter weeks and the race date can reduce training. Starting from zero uses up to three run/walk days; extra available days can be used for walking or recovery.

These are planning rules, not a validated coaching programme or an assessment of injury or race readiness.

Your runs stay on this device

Run data is stored in this browser on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to GitHub or a server. Clearing browser data, changing phones or changing the website address can lose access to it. Export backups regularly.

Apple Watch, Health & Strava

Use Workout on Apple Watch as usual. For a free import, open Health on iPhone → your profile → Export All Health Data, save to Files and unzip. Import export.xml here. Only running and walking workout summaries are read; other Health records are discarded.

You can also import Strava’s activities CSV or GPX/TCX files. Review units and the preview before saving. GPS coordinates are discarded after calculating a distance.

This home-screen website cannot directly read Apple Health. Imports are manual, not automatic sync. Strava currently requires a subscription to register a custom API app; no paid service is needed for the file-import route.
